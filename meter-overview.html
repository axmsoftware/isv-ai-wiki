Why IEEE · why ISV

Rural metering stalls when every vendor ships a closed bundle, tenders copy utility software requirements, and operators cannot swap gear without rewriting billing. IEEE Smart Village sits outside those product lines: neutral place to compare field evidence and publish draft specs without picking a winner. Goal: mixed meter brands on one feeder, and migration paths when a vendor exits.

Why village tenders go wrong

Utility scope on a village budget

Full billing-suite and 15-minute load-profile requirements in a 200-meter tender. City tools; village wallet.

Mixing unlike things

Prepaid tokens, meter reads, inverter monitoring, and cloud format are different layers. One RFP line that says “all protocols” creates impossible bids.

Closed vendor bundles

Mesh + cloud + billing from one vendor ships fast but blocks second-source hardware and migration.

Comms fragility

When pole uplink fails, customers still need tokens and keypads must still work. May 2026 notes.
